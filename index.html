About

Building safe AI for the Global South

I'm an AI safety researcher and senior systems engineer working at the intersection of mechanistic interpretability, clinical AI deployment, and AI governance policy. My research focuses on making AI systems safe and beneficial for underserved populations across East Africa and the Global South.

A Google DeepMind Scholar with First Class Honours in both my BSc and MSc from Makerere University, I've spent over 7 years building enterprise systems used by 10,000+ users across 175+ universities on the continent. My work spans from identifying spurious attention circuits in clinical Vision Transformers to advising the East African Community on regional AI governance frameworks.

Research

Active Research Projects

2026 — Present
AI Safety: Mechanistic Interpretability in Clinical AI
Identified spurious attention circuit (L8H10) in a Vision Transformer trained on chest X-rays — causally verified as harmful yet completely invisible to standard output-level safety evaluation. Direct implications for clinical AI procurement in low-resource settings. Manuscript submitted to Deep Learning Indaba 2026 / IJCAI.
AI Safety Mechanistic Interpretability Vision Transformers
2025 — Present
Pluralistic Alignment: Annotation Bias & Hate Speech
Demonstrated empirically that majority-vote annotation in hate speech datasets suppresses minority value judgments at the hate/offensive boundary — a structural failure with direct implications for multilingual, multicultural populations across East Africa. Accepted at ICML 2026.
